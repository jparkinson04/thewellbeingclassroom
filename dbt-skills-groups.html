Who the groups are for

The programme supports young people experiencing:

  • Relationship difficulties

Skills taught

Across the programme, young people build four core DBT skill sets they can use in school, at home and beyond:

  • Mindfulness
  • Distress Tolerance
  • Emotion Regulation
  • Interpersonal Effectiveness
