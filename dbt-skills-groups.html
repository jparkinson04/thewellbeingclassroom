DBT Skills Groups

An Emotional Regulation Programme that gives young people real skills.

An evidence-informed programme delivered in school by a DBT-informed practitioner and BACP Registered Counsellor, for the young people who need more than a one-off workshop.

Who the groups are for

The programme supports young people experiencing:

Emotional dysregulation Self-harming behaviours Anxiety Impulsivity Relationship difficulties

The four skills taught

Across the programme, young people build four core DBT skill sets they can use in school, at home and beyond.

1

Mindfulness

Noticing thoughts and feelings, and staying present.

2

Distress Tolerance

Getting through difficult moments safely.

3

Emotion Regulation

Understanding and steadying big feelings.

4

Interpersonal Effectiveness

Speaking up, saying no and keeping relationships healthy.
