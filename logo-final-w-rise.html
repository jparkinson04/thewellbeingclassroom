The W Rise — final mark

A "W" for Wellbeing, drawn as one continuous line: it dips, recovers, and ends higher than it began — with a single pink point marking where you arrive. Refined from the concept: the valleys now sit level, the three peaks step upward evenly, and the stroke weight is tuned so the mark holds together from a conference banner down to a browser tab.

Usage rules

  • Keep clear space around the mark of at least the height of one peak.
  • Use the badge version for favicons, app icons and social avatars.
  • Use mono ink when printing single-colour (letterheads, stamps).
  • Pair with Bricolage Grotesque for the name; small caps for the strapline.
  • Don't rotate, outline, add shadows or gradients to the mark.
  • Don't recolour the line — only the dot changes between variants.
  • Don't use below 16px without the badge container.
  • Don't place on busy photos without a paper or ink panel behind it.
