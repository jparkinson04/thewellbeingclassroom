Bespoke: Exploring Misogyny & the Influence of Online Culture

A tailored workshop that encourages young people to critically explore the impact of misogyny, harmful online influences and gender stereotypes. Through open discussion and practical activities, students are supported to understand what misogyny is and how it can develop; recognise the influence of social media, online personalities and algorithms; challenge harmful beliefs and stereotypes respectfully; develop empathy, emotional awareness and critical thinking; build healthy, respectful relationships and positive communication skills; and understand the importance of consent, equality and mutual respect. Adaptable for different age groups — as a single session or part of a wider relationships, wellbeing or PSHE programme.
